Scoring rubric

For all dimensions, the score is 1–10, where 5 means average, “just normal”.

Story/Lore
Story is what the game actively shows you. Example: Mario rescues Peach. Lore is the underlying background setting of the world, as well as untold NPC background, etc. The score reflects how well the game tells the story/lore, and how good the story/lore is. 5 means the story/lore does not add anything to the game, nor undermines it. Example of 5: Overwatch...
Gameplay
The score reflects how fun the game plays. This is a really hard dimension, because for a lot of games it intertwines with all other dimensions. For example for Doki Doki Literature Club, the game play does not come from you actively operating the character, but the story itself. For these games the game play score could be an overall reflection of these dimensions. For other “normal” games that the player actively engages with, this score reflects how good the control is, how impactful the actions are, how clever the puzzles are, etc.
Graphics
This dimension is scored “in context”. I.e. Zelda Breath of the Wild would get a 10, not because it has better graphics than Control on PC, but because it reflects the best balance between style and performance on the Switch. In the same spirit, 2D games are not scored by the same standards as 3D games are.
Audio
This score reflects how good the audio effects and ambience are, as well as how good the music is, if any.
Special
This is a completely subjective dimension. It reflects how I personally feel about a game. Example: Xenoblade Chronicles 2 gets a 10, because it has to be the bestest game ever created, otherwise there is something wrong with the fundamental laws of the universe, meh meh! 5 means I do not feel anything special about this game.
Overall
This is a weighted average of the dimensions above. Currently gameplay weighs twice as much as other dimensions. Ideally, if I randomly sample games, the average overall score should be 5. But obviously in this sheet the average will be greater than 5, because I tend not to play bad games. But in any case, a game scored greater than 5 should be a commendable one.
